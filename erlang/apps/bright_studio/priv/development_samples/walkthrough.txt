Your Bright Studio walkthrough

1. Open Library and choose a source.
2. Read its text in Reader.
3. Select a quote or use Find quote with the keyboard.
4. Add a note and save the evidence.
5. Link evidence to a project board.

A useful note connects a quote to a question or an action.
Evidence keeps the exact source revision that you read.

Try editing a note, viewing its history, and reopening it from a board.
Free development demo data can reset; keep copies of important work.
